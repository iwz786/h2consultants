# Week 1 Onboarding Plan — GTA Deli Packaging
## Day-by-Day Training Schedule for New Sales Representative

---

## OVERVIEW

This 5-day onboarding plan transitions your new hire from zero to confident calling by Friday. Each day builds on the previous. By the end of Week 1, they should be making independent calls with competence and logging results in the CRM.

---

## PRE-ARRIVAL (Before Day 1)

**Manager/Owner completes before the rep starts:**

- [ ] Create company email account for the rep
- [ ] Set up CRM spreadsheet and share access (Google Sheets)
- [ ] Print and package the full training package
- [ ] Prepare sample products (one of each key SKU) in a bag/box
- [ ] Prepare a printed price list and product catalogue
- [ ] Order business cards (rush if needed)
- [ ] Set up their calling workspace (headset, phone, desk, notepad)
- [ ] Prepare a list of 50 warm leads for them to start with (pre-researched)
- [ ] Prepare compensation agreement to sign Day 1

---

## DAY 1 — MONDAY: Welcome, Product Training & Brand Overview

**Goal:** Rep understands the company, the products, and why customers buy from us.

---

### Morning (9:00 AM – 12:00 PM)

**9:00 AM — Welcome & Orientation (30 min)**
- Tour of the workspace (physical or virtual)
- Introduce to team members
- Sign employment documents and commission agreement
- Set up their email and CRM access

**9:30 AM — Company Overview (45 min)**
- History, mission, and values
- The GTA market and our positioning
- Current customers and success stories
- Competitors and how we differentiate

**10:15 AM — Product Deep Dive: Session 1 (90 min)**
Cover products from `07_Sales_Rep_Training_Manual.md` Section A:
- Deli Containers (all sizes, materials, use cases)
- Sandwich Wraps & Deli Paper
- Pass around actual product samples — let them handle each one
- Quiz: What size container is best for a portion of deli salad? For soup?

---

### Afternoon (1:00 PM – 5:00 PM)

**1:00 PM — Product Deep Dive: Session 2 (90 min)**
- Food Trays & Clamshells
- Vacuum Seal Bags
- Eco-Friendly Packaging
- Custom Printed Packaging
- Continue with product samples

**2:30 PM — Review Material & Self-Study (60 min)**
- Read `01_Target_Business_Categories.md` in full
- Read `02_Lead_Sourcing_Guide.md`
- Highlight questions for clarification

**3:30 PM — Q&A + End of Day Debrief (30 min)**
- Answer all product questions
- Assign evening homework: memorize the 6 product categories and their top 3 selling points each

**Day 1 Deliverable:** Rep can name all product categories and one key selling point for each without looking at notes.

---

## DAY 2 — TUESDAY: Scripts, Objections & Role-Play

**Goal:** Rep has internalized the call script and can handle the 7 most common objections convincingly.

---

### Morning (9:00 AM – 12:00 PM)

**9:00 AM — Script Review (60 min)**
- Read through `03_Call_Script_Framework.md` together
- Discuss each section: Opening, Discovery, Value Proposition, Closing
- Manager explains tone and pacing (reference Section B of Training Manual)

**10:00 AM — Script Practice: Solo (30 min)**
- Rep reads the opening script out loud to themselves — 10 times
- Record themselves on phone and listen back once

**10:30 AM — Role-Play Round 1: Opening & Discovery (60 min)**
Manager plays various prospects:
- Scenario A: Friendly restaurant owner, willing to talk
- Scenario B: Busy deli owner who says "I have 2 minutes"
- Scenario C: Gatekeeper at a grocery store
Run each scenario 2–3 times. Give feedback after each.

---

### Afternoon (1:00 PM – 5:00 PM)

**1:00 PM — Objection Handling Deep Dive (60 min)**
- Review each objection in `03_Call_Script_Framework.md` Section D
- Rep reads each objection response OUT LOUD
- Discuss WHY each response works

**2:00 PM — Role-Play Round 2: Full Call Simulations (90 min)**
Run complete call simulations from opening to close:
- Scenario A: Prospect is interested, soft close to email
- Scenario B: "We already have a supplier" objection
- Scenario C: "Send me an email" — book the follow-up
- Scenario D: Meeting booking close
Each scenario 2x. Feedback after every call.

**3:30 PM — Confidence Builder Exercise (30 min)**
- Rep writes their "Why I'm good at this" list (5 reasons they'll succeed)
- Manager affirms and adds to the list

**Day 2 Deliverable:** Rep can complete a full simulated call without referring to notes. They handle at least 3 objections smoothly.

---

## DAY 3 — WEDNESDAY: First Supervised Calls

**Goal:** Rep makes their first real phone calls with manager present for support and immediate feedback.

---

### Morning (9:00 AM – 12:00 PM)

**9:00 AM — CRM Setup & Lead Prep (60 min)**
- Import `05_CRM_Tracking/Call_Tracking_Template.csv` to Google Sheets
- Review the 50 pre-researched leads together
- Set up call tracking columns and drop-downs (follow `Activity_Goals.md`)
- Identify top 20 leads to call today (Priority 1 businesses first)

**10:00 AM — Shadowing Calls (if manager makes calls) (60 min)**
Manager makes 5–8 calls with rep listening on speaker/headset
After each call, discuss what worked, what the rep noticed

---

### Afternoon (1:00 PM – 5:00 PM)

**1:00 PM — First Supervised Calls (90 min)**
Rep makes calls; manager listens without interrupting
- Start with 15 dials
- Log every call in CRM immediately after
- Manager gives brief feedback between every 5 calls (post-call, not during)

**2:30 PM — Mid-Afternoon Debrief (30 min)**
- What surprised them?
- What felt natural? What felt awkward?
- Which objection came up most? Review the response together

**3:00 PM — Continue Calling (60 min)**
- 15 more dials, more independent this time
- Manager present but less hands-on

**4:00 PM — End of Day Debrief (60 min)**
- Review call log together
- Count: dials made, live contacts, interested, voicemails
- Celebrate any wins (a good conversation counts as a win)
- Assign: "Tomorrow morning, you open the CRM and call your 5 most urgent follow-ups before 10 AM"

**Day 3 Deliverable:** Rep has made 30+ dials, logged all calls in CRM, and had at least 5 live conversations.

---

## DAY 4 — THURSDAY: Independent Calling With Check-Ins

**Goal:** Rep operates independently with scheduled check-ins. Builds confidence and self-sufficiency.

---

### Schedule

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 9:00 AM | Rep reviews CRM, plans call list independently |
| 9:30 AM | **10-minute check-in with manager** — what's the plan for the day? |
| 9:45 AM–12:00 PM | Independent calling block (target: 30 dials) |
| 12:00 PM | Lunch — rep self-reviews notes |
| 12:30 PM | **15-minute mid-day check-in** — how is it going? Any stuck objections? |
| 12:45 PM–4:00 PM | Independent calling block (target: 25+ dials) |
| 4:00 PM | Update CRM fully |
| 4:30 PM | **30-minute end-of-day review with manager** |

### End-of-Day Review Agenda:
1. Stats: dials, contacts, interested, voicemails, emails sent
2. Highlight: best call of the day (what went well?)
3. Challenge: toughest objection faced — work through it together
4. Tomorrow: top 5 priority callbacks identified

**Day 4 Deliverable:** Rep makes 50+ dials independently, logs correctly in CRM, identifies at least 2 "Interested" leads.

---

## DAY 5 — FRIDAY: Review, Feedback & Goal Setting

**Goal:** Consolidate week 1 learning. Set clear Month 1 targets. Send rep into Week 2 with confidence and a plan.

---

### Morning (9:00 AM – 12:00 PM)

**9:00 AM — Week 1 Stats Review (60 min)**
Pull from CRM and review together:
- Total dials made
- Live contacts reached
- Contact rate %
- Emails sent
- Interested leads
- Meetings booked (if any)
- CRM setup quality

Compare vs. Week 1 targets in `05_CRM_Tracking/Activity_Goals.md`

**10:00 AM — Skills Assessment (60 min)**
Brief role-play: manager plays 3 different prospect types
- Rep handles opening, discovery, objection, and close without prompting
- Manager scores on: tone, pacing, objection handling, closing attempt

---

### Afternoon (1:00 PM – 3:00 PM)

**1:00 PM — Performance Feedback (30 min)**
- Written feedback on Week 1 (manager prepares brief sheet)
- What's strong? What needs work?
- 1–2 concrete things to focus on in Week 2

**1:30 PM — Month 1 Goal Setting (60 min)**
Co-create personal targets for Month 1:
- Daily dials target
- Weekly contact target
- Meetings per week
- First closed account by [date]
- Revenue contribution target (refer to `08_Incentive_Commission_Structure.md`)

**2:30 PM — Week 2 Plan (30 min)**
- Review lead pipeline — who needs follow-up Monday?
- Identify fastest path to first closed sale
- Any additional training needed (specific objection, product knowledge gap)?

**3:00 PM — Celebration & Motivation**
Acknowledge completing Week 1. Express confidence in the rep's ability.
