## QUICK START CHECKLIST FOR SALES REP

### Before Your First Call
- [ ] Read `07_Sales_Rep_Training_Manual.md` (Product Knowledge section)
- [ ] Study `01_Target_Business_Categories.md`
- [ ] Practice the opening script in `03_Call_Script_Framework.md` out loud 10 times
- [ ] Set up `05_CRM_Tracking/Call_Tracking_Template.csv` in Google Sheets
- [ ] Save email templates to your email client drafts
- [ ] Review objection responses in Section D of the call script

### Week 1 Priorities
1. Complete the `09_Week1_Onboarding_Plan.md` schedule
2. Target **Primary Category** businesses first
3. Aim for 30–40 dials per day
4. Log every call in the CRM tracker
5. Review metrics every Friday
