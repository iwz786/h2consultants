# Introduction Letter — GTA Deli Packaging
## Formal Business Introduction Letter (1 Page)

---

> **Usage:** Print on company letterhead and mail, or attach as PDF to email. Personalize the recipient name and business name for each send. Sign manually for mailed versions.

---

## PRINTING SPECIFICATIONS

| Element | Specification |
|---------|--------------|
| Paper | 24 lb white bond or company letterhead paper |
| Printer | Laser printer preferred (no ink smearing) |
| Letterhead | Print company name/logo/contact top of page |
| Signature | Blue pen — handwritten for mailed versions |
| Envelope | #10 business envelope with company return address |
| Enclosures | Fold pricing sheet and/or flyer inside |

---

*Personalize the recipient name and business name before every send. Never send a generic "Dear Sir/Madam" letter — it signals a mass mail drop.*
