## SUBJECT LINE OPTIONS

Choose based on context:

| Scenario | Subject Line |
|----------|-------------|
| After a positive call | "Great speaking with you — our packaging info as promised" |
| Cold email (no prior call) | "Local packaging supplier for [Business Type] in [Their City]" |
| Referral | "[Referral Name] suggested I reach out — GTA packaging supplier" |
| Follow-up (no response) | "Quick follow-up — [Company Name] packaging" |
| Re-engagement | "Still here when you're ready — [Company Name]" |
| Intro discount | "New customer offer — GTA food packaging supplier" |

```

As promised, here's a quick overview of who we are and what we offer:

We're [Company Name], a GTA-based food packaging supplier serving restaurants, delis, caterers, and food businesses across the Greater Toronto Area.

WHAT WE OFFER:
- Deli containers (various sizes and materials)
- Sandwich wraps and deli paper
- Food trays and clamshell containers
- Vacuum seal bags
- Custom printed packaging (your logo!)
- Eco-friendly options

WHY BUSINESSES CHOOSE US:
✓ Food-grade certified — fully compliant with Canadian food safety standards
✓ Competitive wholesale pricing — typically 15–30% less than retail supply stores
✓ Fast GTA delivery — no waiting weeks for overseas shipments
✓ Flexible minimums — start small, scale as you grow
✓ Custom branding available — your logo on your packaging

NEXT STEPS:

In the meantime, feel free to reply to this email or call me directly at [your phone number].

I've attached our product overview sheet for your reference.

Looking forward to working together,

[Your Full Name]
Sales Representative
[Company Name]
[Phone Number]
[Email]
[Website URL]

Serving Toronto, Mississauga, Brampton, Scarborough, Vaughan & surrounding areas
```

## TEMPLATE 2 — COLD EMAIL (NO PRIOR CALL)

### Plain Text Version:

```
Subject: Local packaging supplier for [delis/restaurants/caterers] in [their city]

Hi [Name / "there" if unknown],

My name is [Your Name] from [Company Name] — we're a GTA-based food packaging supplier working with [restaurants, delis, caterers, grocery stores] across the Greater Toronto Area.

I'm reaching out because we specialize in exactly what businesses like yours go through every day: deli containers, food trays, sandwich wraps, vacuum seal bags, and custom-printed packaging.

Here's what makes us different:
• Food-grade certified products
• Wholesale pricing — 15–30% below retail
• Fast local delivery (no overseas delays)
• No huge minimum orders
• Custom branding with your logo

Would you be open to a quick 5-minute call this week to see if it's a fit? I can also send our pricing sheet if that's easier.

Either way, happy to connect.

Best,
[Your Full Name]
[Phone Number]
[Email]
[Company Name] | [Website]
```
