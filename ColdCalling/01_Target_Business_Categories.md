# Target Business Categories — GTA Deli Packaging
## Prioritized Cold Call Target List

---

## PRIORITY 1 — PRIMARY TARGETS
*Highest volume users | Immediate need | Fast conversion potential*

---

### 1. Delicatessens & Deli Counters
**Why they need us:** Core users of deli containers, wraps, and labelled packaging daily. They package meats, cheeses, salads, and prepared foods constantly.

**Best products to pitch:**
- Deli containers (round/rectangular, various sizes)
- Deli paper and sandwich wraps
- Peel-and-seal labels / printed lid film
- Vacuum seal bags for sliced meats and cheeses

**Pain points to probe:** Running out of stock mid-week, inconsistent quality from current supplier, difficulty getting small reorders, high per-unit costs.

**Search terms:** "deli Toronto", "deli Mississauga", "European deli GTA", "butcher shop GTA"

---

### 2. Restaurants (Dine-in with Takeout/Delivery)
**Why they need us:** Post-pandemic takeout culture means restaurants order packaging constantly. High volume, repeat ordering cycle every 2–4 weeks.

**Best products to pitch:**
- Food trays and clamshell containers
- Soup containers and lids
- Eco-friendly kraft boxes
- Custom printed packaging with their logo

**Pain points to probe:** Generic packaging hurts their brand, current supplier has long lead times, eco-friendly options are too expensive elsewhere.

**Search terms:** "restaurant Toronto", "takeout restaurant Brampton", "Italian restaurant Vaughan takeout"

---

### 3. Catering Companies
**Why they need us:** High-volume orders per event. Often need custom or branded packaging for corporate clients. Bulk buyers with seasonal spikes.

**Best products to pitch:**
- Food trays (half-pan, full-pan)
- Serving containers with lids
- Custom branded packaging for corporate events
- Vacuum seal bags for meal transport

**Pain points to probe:** Inconsistent bulk supply, need for custom branding for high-profile events, balancing cost vs. presentation.

**Search terms:** "catering company Toronto", "corporate catering GTA", "wedding catering Mississauga"

---

### 4. Grocery Stores (Independent & Ethnic)
**Why they need us:** Independent grocery stores with deli, bakery, or prepared foods sections are massive repeat buyers. Ethnic grocers often carry specialty items needing specific packaging.

**Best products to pitch:**
- Deli containers and portion cups
- Food trays for produce and prepared foods
- Vacuum seal bags for meat/fish sections
- Eco-friendly produce packaging

**Pain points to probe:** Buying through a distributor at high markups, inconsistent sizing standards, need for food-grade certified materials.

**Search terms:** "Indian grocery Toronto", "halal grocery Brampton", "independent grocery store GTA", "No Frills affiliated store"

---

### 5. Butcher Shops & Meat Markets
**Why they need us:** Daily packaging of portioned meats. Need vacuum seal bags, trays, and wrapping materials constantly.

**Best products to pitch:**
- Vacuum seal bags (various sizes)
- Foam and plastic meat trays
- Butcher paper and deli wraps
- Freezer-safe labels

**Pain points to probe:** Vacuum bag failures, need for high-clarity packaging to show product quality, sourcing from multiple suppliers.

**Search terms:** "butcher shop Toronto", "halal butcher GTA", "meat market Scarborough", "Portuguese butcher"

---

## PRIORITY 2 — SECONDARY TARGETS
*Good volume | Slightly longer sales cycle | Strong repeat potential*

---

### 6. Food Trucks & Mobile Vendors
**Why they need us:** Order monthly or bi-weekly. Value portability and compact packaging. Often price-sensitive but loyal once converted.

**Best products to pitch:**
- Clamshell takeout containers
- Kraft paper boxes
- Portion cups and lids
- Napkins and serviceware accessories

**Pain points to probe:** Delivery reliability (they can't afford stockouts), pricing, box sizes that fit their specific menu items.

**Search terms:** "food truck Toronto", "food truck permit GTA", "Toronto food truck association"

---

### 7. Meal Prep & Home Delivery Meal Services
**Why they need us:** Fast-growing segment. Need branded, microwave-safe, stackable containers. High repeat volume weekly.

**Best products to pitch:**
- Microwave-safe meal prep containers
- Custom printed labels and lids
- Eco-friendly packaging options
- Insulated delivery bags

**Pain points to probe:** Containers that don't seal well, no custom branding, customers complaining about packaging quality.

**Search terms:** "meal prep delivery Toronto", "weekly meal plan GTA", "healthy meal delivery Mississauga"

---

### 8. Bakeries & Pastry Shops
**Why they need us:** Need window boxes, cake boards, pastry bags, and packaging that showcases product. Presentation-focused buyers.

**Best products to pitch:**
- Bakery boxes with window (various sizes)
- Cake boards and circles
- Custom printed ribbon or stickers
- Eco-kraft options

**Pain points to probe:** Off-brand generic boxes, inability to source seasonal packaging quickly, high minimums from current suppliers.

**Search terms:** "bakery Toronto", "cake shop Mississauga", "patisserie GTA", "custom cakes Brampton"

---

### 9. Sandwich Shops & Sub Franchises (Independent)
**Why they need us:** High daily usage of wraps, bags, and containers. Franchise independents often source locally outside of franchise mandates where possible.

**Best products to pitch:**
- Sandwich wraps and deli paper
- Paper bags and kraft bags
- Drink cups and lids
- Portion containers for toppings/sauces

---

### 10. Schools, Universities & Corporate Cafeterias
**Why they need us:** High-volume institutional buyers. Long contracts, but excellent revenue once landed. Need food-grade certified, often eco-friendly options.

**Best products to pitch:**
- Food trays and compartment containers
- Eco-friendly disposables
- Bulk packaging options

**Contact approach:** Target food service managers and operations directors, not front-line staff.

---

## PRIORITY 3 — TERTIARY OPPORTUNITIES
*Lower immediate volume | Worth nurturing for future | Strategic value*

---

### 11. Hotel Kitchens & Banquet Halls
**Why they need us:** Bulk event packaging needs. Seasonal demand spikes. Once a preferred vendor relationship is established, orders are consistent.

**Best products:** Large food trays, bulk clamshells, custom branded items for events.

---

### 12. Farmer's Markets & Artisan Food Vendors
**Why they need us:** Eco-friendly packaging is a major selling point. Artisans value branding support.

**Best products:** Kraft eco packaging, custom labels, portion cups, jam jar lids.

---

### 13. Cannabis Edibles & Specialty Food Producers
**Why they need us:** Heavily regulated packaging requirements. Need food-grade certified options. Repeat, high-value orders.

**Best products:** Vacuum seals, tamper-evident containers, custom label-ready packaging.

---

### 14. Ghost Kitchens & Cloud Kitchens
**Why they need us:** Delivery-only model means ALL food goes into containers. Multiple brands under one roof = multiple SKUs needed.

**Best products:** Full suite of takeout containers, eco-friendly options, custom printing.

**Search terms:** "ghost kitchen Toronto", "cloud kitchen GTA", "virtual restaurant Toronto"

---

### 15. Hospitals, Long-Term Care & Healthcare Food Services
**Why they need us:** Strict food-grade requirements which we meet. Government procurement, but contract catering companies servicing them are targetable.

**Approach:** Target the catering company that holds the contract, not the institution directly.
