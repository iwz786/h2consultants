# Incentive & Commission Structure — GTA Deli Packaging
## Compensation Plan for Sales Representatives

---

> **Note to Management:** Adjust the base salary, commission rates, and targets below to match your actual margin structure and business goals before presenting this to a sales rep. The figures below are recommended starting points for a GTA B2B sales role in the food supply industry.

---

## COMPENSATION OVERVIEW

| Component | Description | Frequency |
|-----------|-------------|-----------|
| Base Salary | Fixed guaranteed pay | Bi-weekly |
| Commission | % of revenue on closed sales | Monthly |
| New Account Bonus | Bonus per new account acquired | Monthly |
| Spiff (Spot Bonus) | Bonus for specific products or situations | As earned |
| Monthly Target Bonus | Extra pay for hitting % of monthly target | Monthly |
| Quarterly Performance Bonus | Bonus for hitting quarterly goals | Quarterly |

---

## BASE SALARY RECOMMENDATION

| Experience Level | Suggested Base | Notes |
|-----------------|---------------|-------|
| Entry-level (0–1 yr sales) | $38,000–$42,000/yr | ~$1,461–$1,615 bi-weekly |
| Intermediate (1–3 yr experience) | $42,000–$50,000/yr | With demonstrated track record |
| Senior (3+ yr B2B sales) | $50,000–$60,000/yr | If hiring a proven closer |

**OTE (On-Target Earnings):** With base + commission + bonuses, a performer hitting 100% of targets should earn $55,000–$80,000/yr depending on level.

---

## COMMISSION STRUCTURE

### Standard Commission Rate

| Revenue Generated (Monthly) | Commission Rate |
|----------------------------|----------------|
| $0 – $10,000 | 5% |
| $10,001 – $25,000 | 7% |
| $25,001 – $50,000 | 9% |
| $50,001+ | 11% |

Commission is calculated on **gross revenue from closed orders**, not gross profit. Rates can be adjusted if margins are tight — consult accountant.

### Commission Timing
- Commission is paid **monthly**, on the 15th of the following month
- Only paid on **invoiced and paid** orders (not on account receivables outstanding > 60 days)
- If an order is refunded or cancelled, commission is clawed back on the next payment

### Example Commission Calculation

```
Month: May 2026
Total Revenue from Closed Sales: $18,500

$10,000 × 5% = $500
$8,500 × 7% = $595

Total Commission = $1,095
Base Salary (bi-weekly × 2) = $3,076
Total May Compensation = $4,171
```

## NEW ACCOUNT ACQUISITION BONUS (Spiff)

Reward the rep for every net-new paying customer they bring in:

| Condition | Bonus |
|-----------|-------|
| New account places first order ($0–$499) | $25 |
| New account places first order ($500–$999) | $50 |
| New account places first order ($1,000–$2,499) | $75 |
| New account places first order ($2,500+) | $150 |
| New account signs standing order agreement | $200 |

**Payout:** Same month as the first invoice is paid.

**Goal:** Aggressively grow the customer base in year 1.

---

## PRODUCT SPIFFS (Spot Bonuses)

To incentivize specific product pushes:

| Product / Situation | Bonus |
|--------------------|-------|
| First custom printing order (any size) | $100 |
| Eco-friendly line opening order ($500+) | $50 |
| Vacuum seal bag new account (butcher/deli) | $60 |
| Upsell existing account by 30%+ in one month | $75 |
| Booking and attending in-person meeting → closed | $50 |

Management can activate or deactivate spiffs based on inventory/priority. Communicated at monthly team meeting.

## MONTHLY TARGET BONUS

Based on percentage of monthly revenue target achieved:

| Target Achievement | Monthly Bonus |
|--------------------|--------------|
| 80–89% of target | $150 |
| 90–99% of target | $300 |
| 100–109% of target | $500 |
| 110–124% of target | $700 |
| 125%+ of target | $1,000 |

**Monthly Revenue Targets by Ramp Stage:**

| Month | Target |
|-------|--------|
| Month 1 | $3,000 |
| Month 2 | $6,000 |
| Month 3 | $10,000 |
| Month 4–6 | $15,000 |
| Month 7–12 | $20,000–$25,000 |

---

## QUARTERLY PERFORMANCE BONUS

At the end of each quarter (Q1: Jan–Mar, Q2: Apr–Jun, etc.):

| Condition | Bonus |
|-----------|-------|
| Hit 100% of quarterly revenue target | $500 |
| Hit 110% of quarterly revenue target | $1,000 |
| Hit 125%+ of quarterly revenue target | $1,500 |
| Opened 10+ new accounts in the quarter | +$250 |
| Zero customer complaints in the quarter | +$200 |

---

## ANNUAL BONUS

At end of year performance review:

| Condition | Bonus |
|-----------|-------|

| 12-month account retention rate 80%+ | +$500 |

## NON-MONETARY INCENTIVES

Beyond compensation, consider:

- **Recognition:** "Rep of the Month" announcement, public recognition
- **Flexibility:** Work from home days as reward for top performers
- **Career growth:** Path to Senior Rep or Account Manager role
- **Product knowledge trips:** Visit trade shows (Restaurant Canada Show, Toronto food expo)
- **Team competitions:** Weekly leaderboard, small prizes (gift cards, lunch)

---

## COMMISSION AGREEMENT KEY TERMS

When presenting the compensation plan to a new hire, include:

1. Commission is earned on **paid invoices only**
2. Commission on accounts **you opened** — not inherited accounts (in year 1)
3. Commission clawback if order is returned/refunded within 60 days
4. All new accounts must be registered in CRM within 24 hours of first order
5. Monthly targets reviewed and adjusted quarterly based on performance and market conditions
6. This compensation plan may be revised with 30 days' written notice

---

*Get the commission structure confirmed and signed by both the rep and management before the rep's first day.*
