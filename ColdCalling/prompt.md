# Complete Cold Calling Sales Training Package for Deli Packaging Business

I need a comprehensive sales training package for my newly hired cold calling sales representative who will be promoting our GTA-based deli packaging business.

## Business Context
- **Company**: Deli packaging supplier based in Greater Toronto Area
- **Products**: Deli containers, sandwich wraps, food trays, vacuum seal bags, custom printed packaging, eco-friendly options
- **Target Market**: GTA businesses that need food packaging
- **Website**: [provide your website URL]
- **Unique Selling Points**: Food-grade certified, competitive wholesale pricing, fast GTA delivery, custom branding available, flexible minimum orders

## Deliverables Needed

### 1. TARGET BUSINESS CATEGORIES
Create a detailed list of business types to cold call, organized by priority. Include:
- Primary targets (e.g., delis, restaurants, catering companies)
- Secondary targets (e.g., food trucks, meal prep companies)
- Tertiary opportunities
- For each category, explain WHY they need our products and what specific products suit them best

### 2. LEAD SOURCING GUIDE
Provide a step-by-step guide on where and how to find contact information:
- Online directories and databases (free and paid options)
- Google Maps / Google Business strategies
- LinkedIn approaches
- Industry associations
- Local business registries
- Networking events in GTA
- Include specific search terms and filters to use

### 3. COMPLETE CALL SCRIPT FRAMEWORK
Create a professional cold calling script structure including:

**A. Opening Script (First 15 seconds)**
- Attention-grabbing introduction
- Purpose statement
- Permission to continue

**B. Discovery Questions**
- Questions to identify their current packaging supplier
- Pain points to uncover (cost, quality, delivery issues)
- Volume and frequency needs
- Custom branding interests

**C. Value Proposition Delivery**
- How to present our USPs based on their answers
- Specific benefits for their business type
- Differentiation from competitors

**D. Handling Common Objections**
Script responses for:
- "We already have a supplier"
- "We're not interested"
- "Send me information via email"
- "Your prices are too high"
- "I need to think about it"
- "I'm too busy right now"
- "Call me back later"

**E. Closing Techniques**
- How to ask for the sale
- How to book an in-person meeting
- How to secure email/follow-up permission
- Soft close vs. hard close scenarios

**F. Follow-up Protocol**
- What to say on second, third calls
- How to reference previous conversations
- Re-engagement strategies for cold leads

### 4. SALES COLLATERAL PACKAGE
Create the following ready-to-use materials:

**A. Email Introduction Template**
- Professional email to send after calls
- Include: company intro, product overview, USPs, CTA
- HTML email version and plain text version
- Subject line variations for different scenarios

**B. Product Catalogue (Digital PDF)**
Design a professional PDF catalogue including:
- Cover page with branding
- Table of contents
- Product categories with images and descriptions
- Key features and specifications
- Quality certifications
- Customization options
- Contact information
- 8-12 pages, print-ready and email-friendly

**C. Price List Template**
- Professional pricing sheet (Excel + PDF versions)
- Organized by product category
- Volume discount tiers
- Wholesale vs. retail pricing structure
- Terms and conditions footer
- "Subject to change" disclaimer
- Space for custom quotes

**D. One-Page Sales Flyer**
- Quick-reference PDF highlighting:
- Top 5-6 product categories
- Key benefits
- Special offer or intro discount
- Contact details and website
- Visually branded, easy to email or print

**E. Introduction Letter/Brochure**
- Formal business introduction letter (1 page)
- Company background
- Products and services overview
- Why choose us section
- Call to action

### 5. CRM & TRACKING SYSTEM
Provide a simple tracking system:

**A. Call Tracking Spreadsheet Template**
Excel/Google Sheets with columns for:
- Business name
- Contact person & title
- Phone & email
- Business type
- Call date & time
- Call outcome (interested/not interested/callback/meeting booked)
- Follow-up date
- Notes
- Status pipeline (Cold > Contacted > Interested > Meeting > Quoted > Closed)

**B. Daily/Weekly Activity Goals**
- Number of calls per day target
- Contact rate goals
- Meeting booking targets
- Conversion benchmarks

### 6. MEETING BOOKING PROTOCOL
Instructions for when prospects want to meet in person:

**A. Meeting Script**
- How to propose meeting
- Questions to ask before confirming (decision maker, timeline, current pain points)
- Meeting agenda to present

**B. Meeting Confirmation Email Template**
- Professional confirmation message
- Date, time, location
- What to prepare
- Agenda overview

**C. Pre-Meeting Checklist**
What the sales rep should send/bring to meetings:
- Physical samples (if applicable)
- iPad/laptop with website
- Printed catalogues
- Business cards
- Quote forms
- Contract templates
