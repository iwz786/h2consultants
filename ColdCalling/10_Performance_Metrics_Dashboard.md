## KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS — DEFINITIONS

| KPI | Formula | Why It Matters |
|-----|---------|---------------|
| **Dial-to-Contact Rate** | Live contacts ÷ total dials | Measures list quality and calling efficiency |
| **Contact-to-Interest Rate** | Interested leads ÷ live contacts | Measures script effectiveness |
| **Interest-to-Meeting Rate** | Meetings booked ÷ interested leads | Measures closing ability on phone |
| **Meeting-to-Quote Rate** | Quotes sent ÷ meetings held | Measures meeting effectiveness |
| **Quote-to-Close Rate** | Closed deals ÷ quotes sent | Measures negotiation and follow-up |
| **Overall Conversion Rate** | Closed deals ÷ total dials | Overall funnel efficiency |
| **Average Order Value (AOV)** | Total revenue ÷ number of orders | Indicates mix of small vs. large accounts |
| **Customer Acquisition Cost** | Total time investment ÷ new customers | Helps evaluate prospecting efficiency |
| **Account Retention Rate** | Repeat orders ÷ total accounts × 100 | Key health indicator for year 2+ |

## RED FLAGS — WHEN TO INTERVENE

As manager, watch for these warning signs:

| Signal | Threshold | Action |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Dials < 150/week | 2 weeks in a row | Review time management and call blocking |
| Contact rate < 15% | Any week | Review list quality — wrong numbers? Wrong times? |
| Zero meetings booked | 2 weeks in a row | Role-play meeting booking script; listen to calls |
| Quotes sitting > 7 days without follow-up | Any quote | Immediate coaching on follow-up urgency |
| CRM not updated daily | Any week | Accountability conversation; CRM is non-negotiable |
| Rep morale declining | Any sign | 1-on-1 conversation; review wins; address concerns |

## GOOGLE SHEETS DASHBOARD FORMULAS

Add these to your Dashboard tab in the CRM spreadsheet:

```
Total Dials (Week):
=COUNTIF(Sheet1!CallOutcome,"<>")

Live Contacts:
=COUNTIFS(Sheet1!CallOutcome,"<>No Answer",Sheet1!CallOutcome,"<>Voicemail Left",Sheet1!CallOutcome,"<>Wrong Number")

Meetings Booked:
=COUNTIF(Sheet1!MeetingBooked,"Y")

Deals Closed (Won):
=COUNTIF(Sheet1!PipelineStatus,"Closed - Won")

Revenue Closed:
=SUMIF(Sheet1!PipelineStatus,"Closed - Won",Sheet1!QuoteValue)

Active Pipeline Value:
=SUMIFS(Sheet1!QuoteValue,Sheet1!PipelineStatus,"Quoted")

Contact Rate:
=LiveContacts/TotalDials

Close Rate:
=ClosedWon/QuotesSent
```
