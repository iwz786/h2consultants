# Lead Sourcing Guide — GTA Deli Packaging
## Step-by-Step Guide to Finding Prospect Contact Information

---

## OVERVIEW

Your goal: Build a list of 500+ qualified prospects in the GTA before you begin calling. Refresh this list weekly. Quality matters — target decision makers (owners, managers, purchasing officers), not front-line staff.

---

## METHOD 1 — GOOGLE MAPS (HIGHEST VALUE, FREE)

Google Maps is your best free tool for finding local food businesses with contact details.

### Step-by-Step:
1. Go to **maps.google.com**
2. In the search bar, type one of the search terms below
3. Set location to "Greater Toronto Area" or specific city (Toronto, Mississauga, Brampton, Scarborough, Vaughan, Markham, Richmond Hill, Etobicoke)
4. Click through each result — collect: **Business name, phone number, website, address**
5. Click "Website" to find owner name and email if not listed
6. Log directly into your CRM spreadsheet

### GTA Search Terms to Use (run each one):
```
deli Toronto
deli near me [set location to each GTA city]
butcher shop Toronto
butcher shop Mississauga
catering company Toronto
catering GTA
food truck Toronto
meal prep delivery Toronto
restaurant takeout Brampton
grocery store Scarborough
bakery Vaughan
sandwich shop Toronto
ghost kitchen Toronto
```

### Pro Tips:
- Filter by **rating (4+ stars)** — established businesses that care about quality
- Check "Hours" — if they're open 6+ days/week, they have high volume
- Read reviews — reviewers mention packaging, freshness, containers (gives you intel)
- Use **Street View** to see the size of the establishment

- Search by neighbourhood or food type

- Download the full list as a spreadsheet — this is a goldmine
- Filter for "Pass" inspections (active businesses only)

## METHOD 4 — LINKEDIN (BEST FOR CATERING & CORPORATE)

### How to Use LinkedIn Sales Navigator (or Free Search):

#### Free LinkedIn Strategy:
1. Search: `"catering manager" OR "food service manager" Toronto`
2. Search: `"restaurant owner" GTA`
3. Search: `"purchasing manager" food Toronto`
4. Filter by: **Location = Greater Toronto Area**, **Industry = Food & Beverages**
5. Send a personalized connection request (do NOT pitch in the first message)
6. After connecting, message to introduce yourself

#### Connection Request Template:
> Hi [Name], I work with food businesses across the GTA providing packaging solutions. Your [company] caught my eye — I'd love to connect and learn more about your operation. No pitch, just building my network in the industry.

#### LinkedIn Search Strings:
```
"catering" "Toronto" "owner" OR "manager"
"deli" "GTA" "purchasing"
"food truck" "operator" Toronto
"meal prep" "founder" Toronto
"ghost kitchen" Toronto
```

## METHOD 6 — LOCAL BUSINESS REGISTRIES

### Province of Ontario Business Registry:
- **URL**: ontario.ca/page/ontario-business-registry
- Search by business name or industry
- Confirms business is legally registered and active

### City of Toronto Business Licences:
- **URL**: toronto.ca/business-economy/business-permits-licences
- Search restaurant/food business licences
- Cross-reference with DineSafe for full picture

### Peel Region Business Directory:
- **URL**: peelregion.ca (covers Brampton, Mississauga, Caledon)
- Business licence search available
